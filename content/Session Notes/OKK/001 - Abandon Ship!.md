## Summary

On our trip to Cutlass, the ship's captain noticed that we were being followed by pirates. Instead of accepting our
fate, he steered the ship out of the shipping lane, possibly east. We struggled to weather a terrible storm as several
pirates caught us and boarded the ship. (They were searching for something specific.) A major battle occurred between
the Cutlass Marines (our escort) and the pirates. Before it concluded, the ship crashed and wrecked.

We woke up on the tip of a crescent-shaped island. We shared a crab meal with some of the pirates and told them we'd
talk to the others about some of the pirates joining us. We found the ship wreck with 20-something survivors, including
several marines, our starting equipment, and Marine Captain [[Graves]] cursed with Necrotic Death.
