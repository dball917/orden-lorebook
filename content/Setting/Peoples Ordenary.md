The peoples of [[Orden]] whose lineage is from the Prime Material Plane.

## The Progenitor Races

In ancient times, [[Orden]] was populated by three races: the [[Titan|Titans]] who walked the land, the [[Primordial|Primordials]] of the deep seas, and the [[Dragon|Dragons]] who were the masters of the skies. Each race was given immortality and [[Orden]] was in balance, if not always at peace, for millennia. 

As their lives stretched on and on, the progenitors began to resent the restrictions of a world that felt too small and familiar. It is unknown which race first attempted to ascend to godhood, or exactly why, but eventually all three began searching for ways to evolve beyond their physical forms. A byproduct of this was the creation of the lesser races: imperfect mortal representations of the progenitors. Eventually, the progenitors succeeded in their ascension, or at least disappeared from [[Orden]], leaving the lesser races to grow and spread. After many centuries of evolution, [[Orden]] is now populated with many diverse intelligent peoples which trace their histories back to one or more of the progenitors.

## The Lesser Races

In the face of what many still consider to be an invasion of other peoples following the [[Conjunction of Planes]], the Peoples Ordenary largely see themselves as one group. When conflict and war break out among them, it is most often out of cultural or territorial disputes than over the issue of lineage (though this is not _always_ the case). The Peoples Ordinary include:
* [[Human|Humans]]: The most prevalent of the Peoples Ordenary and most likely to build and govern kingdoms and empires
* [[Dwarf|Dwarves]]: Originally a subterranean people, the formation of [[The Shadowed Depths]] pushed many of them their ancestral homes. They were among the first to assimilate with their [[Human]] neighbors
* [[Halfling|Halflings]]: Eschewing both city life and living underground in ancient times, the Halflings split from their [[Human]] and [[Dwarf]] cousins to live in small clans close to nature. That natural connection still lives in many of the smallfolk to this day, even as time has seen them accept more urban lives.
* [[Goliath]]: A sturdy people, the Goliath originally hailed from the mountains and peaks of Orden and are some of the most direct progenitors of the [[Titan]] bloodline, behind perhaps only the [[Giant|Giants]]. There is somewhat of a divide between those that keep to the mountains and the ancient ways and those that have become more cosmopolitan.
* [[Orc|Orcs]]: In ancient times the orcs were known as merciless marauders, with a mind only for the destruction of the civilization of other peoples. In time though, and in the face of mutual peril, their relationships with other peoples stabilized. Many still choose to live nomadic lifestyles in the ways of their ancestors.
* [[Dragonborn]]: Unlike most of the other Peoples Ordenary, the Dragonborn have remained insulated and aloof, preferring only to keep close ties to other peoples of [[Dragon]] blood (like [[Kobold|Kobolds]]). Renowned for their unique [[Artifice]] that allows for wonders such as flying ships and even cities, they jealously guard their secrets that make them the masters of the sky.

Species from the Peoples Ordenary that play lesser roles in world affairs include: [[Giant|Giants]], [[Lizardfolk]], [[Merfolk]], [[Kobolds]], [[Bullywugs]] and similar more "monstrous" peoples.
