Ageron's calendar is the standard calendar system of [[Orden]]. It is named for a figure in the Old [[Empire of Crest]] whose actual identity is lost to history but is said to have been a powerful wizard.

It's a sidereal solar calendar, that uses the position of certain constellations in the sky to keep track of time. 

## Structure

This calendar has 12 months, each comprised of 30 days, with a 5 to 6 (depending on the year) liminal days inserted between the months.
