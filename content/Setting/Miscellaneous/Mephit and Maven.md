The *Mephit and Maven* was a cargo and passenger ship out of [[Cutlass]] that made frequent crossings of the [[Kraken's Maw]].

## Sinking

The *Mephit and Maven* sunk in 1145 PCE when it was simultaneously struck by a massive storm during a crossing of the [[Kraken's Maw]] and attacked by [[The Red Scorpion]] and his crew, notorious [[Pirates of the Maw|pirates]]. At the time it was also carrying a small force of [[Ironwood Concordant Marine Corp]] officers led by Captain [[Claiborne Grimes]]. 

The circumstances of the sinking, and why [[The Red Scorpion]] was pursuing it, are still mysterious.

## Survivors

Following its sinking, the wreckage washed up on an unidentified island in the Maw.
