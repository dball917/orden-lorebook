The Kraken's Maw is a sea that separate the northern kingdoms of [[Crestfall]] from the [[Ironwood Concordant]] in the south. It's history and impact on modern society are well documented by the scribes of the [[The Crucible]].

> To those of us that know her best, the Maw is both welcome friend and unpredictable, deadly adversary. Her weather and tides shift and change as quickly as a sailor's favorite whore. I'm not actually sure which of the two have killed more cadets. 
> 							*- Corporal "Jolly" Jon Hayden, Instructor at [[The Crucible]]*

## Document No. 4295 - Primer on the Kraken's Maw 

### Introduction
As a Marine, your life is going to revolve around the Maw, so it's best you know everything you can about it before you even sniff the deck of a ship. If you grew up here in [[Cutlass]], I doubt I'm telling you anything new, but this should catch up those of you from deeper into the [[Ironwood Concordant|Concordant]].

First, the basics. The Maw is first and foremost our best line of defense from the northern kingdoms and their endless ambitions. Our ability to make the most of that advantage is directly related to our continued naval dominance and the skill and bravery of young folks like yourselves. It is also vitally important for trade, but I'll leave the specifics on that  for a more appropriate instructor.

The Maw is named, as you've likely heard, for its hundreds of small islands which have been said to resemble the teeth in a Kraken's maw. Most of those islands are unexplored and uninhabited, at least by anything you'd like to meet. [[Cutlass]] is, by far, the largest city on its coast but it's not the only one. There are many villages on the southern coast and some of the closer islands, including [[Fisher's Rest]], which is becoming something of a bustling port itself these days. The northern and western coasts are less populated, as the coastline is mountainous and not ideal for landings. However, the ports of [[Lashport]] in [[Crestfillia]] and [[Bennet's Landing]] in [[Trill]] are important for trade and travel to the North.

### Formation
During the time of the [[Empire of Crest]], the Maw was actually a stretch of land known for its rivers, vibrant towns, marshy lowlands. The region allowed land travel from [[Capital]] and its surrounding lands down to the [[Ironwood Forrest]] and [[Orclands]]. It was this route that brought the [[Empire of Crest|Empire's]] armies south to take [[Blackbark]] and subjugate [[Fort Prosperity]] and the [[Orc|Orcish]] lands. It was also the home of the third largest city in the Empire, a thriving port called [[Trident]], the gateway to the [[Eastern Continents]]. 

As best as anyone can tell, around 640 PCE records began to emerge with reports of increased seismic activity in the region. At first, the tremors were rather small, not too dissimilar to the little shakes we get here every now and again. However, over the next few years they began to get stronger and more violent. Then, in 647, an enormous quake rocked the entire region, toppling cities and opening fissures in the ground in an event we now know as the [[FIrst Cataclysm]]. Already a wet, low elevation area, the fissures allowed water to bubble up and turned the entire region into a gigantic sinkhole. When the land began to collapse, water rushed in from the [[Tractless Sea]] in a massive flooding event. The result of all that is the Kraken's Maw, and it hasn't gotten any safer since then.
### Dangers of the Maw
There's plenty of things that will get you killed when sailing the Maw. These are the ones you're most likely to encounter:
* **Weather**: There is bad weather, and then there is Maw weather. Not only is it almost impossible to predict when a storm will hit, the damn things are so invested in magic that I've seen those sensitive to the mystic arts pass out just by us getting close. And Titans preserve anyone unlucky enough to get caught up inside one for very long. Fortunately, they seem worse in the center of the Maw and more manageable near the coastlines. Remember to stay near the coast.
* **Pirates**: We aren't alone out on the waters. There are gangs of lawless thugs out there who'd love nothing more than to slit the throat of every decent person and rob them blind. We call these villains the [[Pirates of the Maw]] and they're harder to get rid of than a nest of Giant Rats. Half our job is escorting merchants to protect them from these ruffians. Gods only know how they survive out there, there are even rumors of an entire city of Pirates out there in the isles, though I personally don't believe it's real.
* **Monsters**: If all that wasn't bad enough, there's the Sahuagin to worry about. And the Merfolk. And all the tenticley things. There have even been Dragon Turtle sightings. And those are just the things where the ship that encountered them came back to file the reports.
