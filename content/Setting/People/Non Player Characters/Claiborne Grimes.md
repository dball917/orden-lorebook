Claiborne Grimes is an NPC in the Of Kingdoms and Krakens campaign. He is a Captain in the [[Ironwood Concordant Marine Corp]] and is a well known figure in the region.

## Overview
* Male Human
* Captain in the [[Ironwood Concordant Marine Corp]]
* Last Known Location: Wreck site of the *[[Mephit and Maven]]*, on an unknown island in the [[Kraken's Maw]]

## Details

### Aboard the Mephit and Maven

Grimes led a contingent of [[Ironwood Concordant Marine Corp|marines]] on an undisclosed mission aboard the [[Mephit and Maven]] in 1145 PCE. When the ship was attacked by [[The Red Scorpion]] he fought the pirate captain seemingly to a draw. The battering of the ship, however gave the pirate an opening and Grimes was wounded in the exchange.

## Shipwrecked

Grimes was one of a score of survivors of the wreck and was marooned with them on an unknown island in the [[Kraken's Maw]]. The wound he took during the battle turned out to be from an enchanted weapon favored by [[The Red Scorpion]] which imparts the [[curse of Rotting Death]], preventing him from recovering without. He is currently incapacitated and being tended to by the other survivors at the wreck site.
