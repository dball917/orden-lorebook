## Eras and Ages

Our history is varied, rich, and ancient, stretching back to our [[Titan|Titanic]] ancestors. To impress its complexities onto your young, ignorant minds would be folly of the highest degree. We have, therefor, designed this simplified model for students of all disciplines in the hopes of at least dispelling some of the local mysticism and inaccurate historical understanding of your birthplaces. 

Therefore, history can be thought of broadly in terms of **Eras** and **Ages**. The former is delineated by events of such great import that they changed life on [[Orden]] forever. This includes the [[Progenitor Exodus]] and, of course, the [[Conjunction of Planes]]. **Ages** then, represent a further separation into groups largely defined by shifts in culture or political power but sharing the same assumptions and context of their era. Students who undertake the grand study of History will no doubt come to find, in due time, these generalizations insultingly simplistic.

## Description of Eras

Once again, I feel it incumbent upon me to impress on the reader that the intent of this treatise is to provide the barest of understanding for the most average of students and not as an authoritative record of note. The dates and descriptions have been annotated to give the reader a basic understanding of the time periods and a frame of reference for when certain events they may encounter in their studies occurred in relation to others. However, especially in the elder ages, the concept of record keeping (and indeed even the passage of time itself) varied greatly from how we experience it today. Our progenitors were, after all, immortal (after a a fashion).

### The Prehistoric Era
**Dates:** Uncatalogued

As the name implies, the Prehistoric Era exists before any sense of historical record on [[Orden]] and encompasses all time before the known existence of any of the [[Peoples Ordenary#The Progenitor Races|Progenitor Races]]. We have almost no knowledge of these times other than that logic dictates that it must, in fact, exist.

### The Progenitor Era
**Dates:** 0 - 9,000 PE

The time when [[Orden]] was rules over by the [[Peoples Ordenary#The Progenitor Races|Progenitor Races]]: the [[Titan|Titans]], [[Dragon|Dragons]], and [[Primordial|Primordials]]. As indicated in this chapter's introduction, the progenitors did not experience time in the same way as we do, making it nearly impossible to attribute specific events to specific dates, at least in the traditional sense. Date in this era, therefore, are relative to _one another_ more so than they are to traditional years in our [[Ageron's Calendar|calendar]]. It is better to think of the Ages below as arbitrary ranges of time where lower dates are _reasonably assumed_ to have occurred before higher ones. Then duration of 9,000 years was chosen to give this period a sense of scale understandable by today's reckoning but that too is almost assuredly inaccurate.

#### The Age of Conflict (PE 0 - 2,999)
When the progenitor races were young, and potentially still undergoing some form of evolution, there was much conflict. These conflicts shaped much of the world as we knew it [[Conjunction of Planes|Pre-Conjunction]] and many of the features that still exist to this day. Records (well, religious and mythological accounts, really) tell us that this time was marked with constant war and squabbling both between and among the races.

#### The Age of Balance (PE 3,000 - 5,999)
At some point, the [[Peoples Ordenary#The Progenitor Races|Progenitor Races]] grew tired of their endless fighting and conflict and forged an uneasy peace. This peace formed into a Balance that allowed the world to rest and develop. Absent the conflict the races were able to grow and develop skills and knowledge. It was late during this time when [[Peoples Ordenary#The Lesser Races|the Lesser Races]] began to be formed.

#### The Age of Exodus (PE 6,000 - 9,000)
At some point, the progenitors grew tired of this world and their endlessness and sought ways to leave [[Orden]], we assume to ascend to some higher plane. Distracted by this goal, they began to leave more and more to the [[Peoples Ordenary#The Lesser Races|the Lesser Races]] who were beginning to become more diverse. In these times the distinctions between races like [[Human|Humans]] and [[Dwarf||Dwarves]] started to become distinct. We don't know much about the [[Progenitor Exodus]], how they managed it or where they went, but it is a fact that they eventually succeeded and left this world.

### The Lesser Ordenary Era
**Dates:** 1 - 2815 LOE

This Era comprises the time between the [[Progenitor Exodus]] and the [[Conjunction of Planes]] when the [[Peoples Ordenary]] were the sole inhabitants of [[Orden]]. This is when scholars such as myself can being to piece together a more accurate history as our ancestors began to record time and events in a more modern way. The earliest instance of the usage of dates arises from this era in the works of [[Helmut to Lesser]] and his [[Anthology of True Events]]  which was written in 500 LOE and refers to the [[Progenitor Exodus]] and when it occurred relative to his time.
