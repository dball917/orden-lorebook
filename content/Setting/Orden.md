Orden is the name of the world where we play D&D. In antiquity, an event called the [[Conjunction of Planes]] occurred, which fused parts of the Prime Material Plan with the Feywild and Shadowfell. It exists in an uneasy balance between the natives of this Planes and is of great interest to [[Outsider|Outsiders]] and denizens of the [[Multiverse]].

## Regions of Orden (work in progress)

The following Regions exist in the world of Orden:
* [[Crestfall]]: Former home to the great [[Human]] [[Empire of Crest]], it is now comprised of several nations and city-states forged from the shards of its collapse. 
* [[Feylands]]: The current home of the [[Elf|Elves]] and their allies the [[Gnome|Gnomes]] it is a slice of the Feywild made material. It is isolated by large chasms surrounding the area and is most easily accessed via the air or the [[Great City of Glaive]] 
* [[The Scaled Expanse]]: Said to be the home of the [[Dragonborn]] and their floating cities, this inhospitable desert is fraught with mystery and danger, but not without its opportunities for adventure and profit
* [[The Shadowed Depths]]: Underground region that resulted from the [[Conjunction of Planes|conjunction]] of the Underdark and the Shadowfell

## Peoples of Orden

The peoples of Orden are especially diverse, given their varied origins. While many exist harmoniously, that is far from universally true. They can largely be grouped based on what plane they originated from during the [[Conjunction of Planes]]:
* [[Peoples Ordenary]]: hail from the Prime Material Plane
* [[Peoples Fey]]: originate form the Feywild
* [[Peoples of Shadows]]: trace lineage to the lands of the Shadowfell
* [[Outsider|Outsiders]]: visitors from other planes and places in the [[Multiverse]]
