## Tone
The campaign is like _Pirates of the Caribbean_ with some of the fantastical feel of the _One Piece_ world and your typical _D&D_ tropes, but  with political undertones from the larger world/setting. 

It is a Swashbuckling Adventure set on the seas and islands of an Archipelago called the [[Kraken's Maw]], which is essentially a lawless wilderness. Players should expect sword fights, ship battles, exploring mysterious islands, and treasure hunts as they navigate the region's various powers and their proxies who will be after the same things.  And pirates!

## Themes

The following Themes should be considered when creating/developing characters for this campaign:
### Freedom
The world outside the [[Kraken's Maw|Maw]] is *civilized*. Civilized society is structured, ordered, and oppressive to those who don't fit in. Player Characters should have reasons to seek reprieve from this in some way so that a life outside of its structure would be appealing. Otherwise they should have a _strong_ reason for staying put despite their preferences.
### Choices over Solutions
The players will be given problems without a single, obviously correct solution and will be asked to make decisions based on their character's worldview. This means a characters will _need_ a worldview; at least enough of one to guide decision making. Attempt to avoid anachronisms based on modern, America-centric thinking. 
### Pulpy but Serious
As any good swashbuckling adventure should be high on pulp and camp, those types of traits are encouraged in characters. However, they shouldn't be one-note goofy or joke characters. In other words, characters should have some depth to them even if they have quirky characteristics.
